A Rich History

Sholo Guti has roots that stretch back hundreds of years. It was documented by British civil servant Henry Parker in 1909 in his book "Ancient Ceylon," where he called it "Hewakam Keliya" — The War Game.

In villages across Bangladesh, India, and Sri Lanka, people still draw the board in dirt, use stones as pieces, and play for hours under the shade of trees. This digital version carries that same spirit into your pocket.

The game belongs to a family of leap-capture games that includes Alquerque from the Middle East, Draughts from Europe, and related games like Peralikatuma and Kotu Ellima from Sri Lanka. Each variant tells a story of how strategy travels across cultures and generations.
